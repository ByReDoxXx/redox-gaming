# RedoX Gaming Wiki

RedoX Gaming is a free, open-source retro game player for **Windows, Linux, Android TV and Android phones**. It plays 13 classic systems through libretro emulator cores, has a pixel-art interface made for the TV remote and the gamepad, and comes with a store of free, legal homebrew games.
